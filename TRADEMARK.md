## 3. Permitted Use

You may use the protected identifiers in the following limited ways:

### 3.1 Accurate Attribution

You may state that your work references, quotes, or is based on Maat-Cyber-World, provided the statement is truthful and includes a link to the Official Project.

Example:

> Based on material from Maat-Cyber-World by Maat.  
> https://github.com/Maat-Cyber/Maat-Cyber-World

<br/>

### 3.2 Educational Reference

You may mention the project in educational discussions, videos, articles, CTF writeups, lectures, or security community discussions, provided you do not imply official endorsement.

<br/>

### 3.3 Contribution Forks

You may use GitHub’s standard fork feature to contribute pull requests to the Official Project.

Contribution forks must not present themselves as the official repository and must not attempt to collect subscribers, users, donations, credentials, or community members under the Maat-Cyber-World identity.

<br/>
<br/>

## 4. Prohibited Use

You may not do any of the following:

### 4.1 Fake Copies

You may not clone, mirror, scrape, fork, republish, or recreate this repository, website, GitBook, documentation, or branding to create a fake or misleading copy of the Official Project.

<br/>

### 4.2 Subscriber or User Harvesting

You may not use the Maat-Cyber-World name, branding, or content to collect or attempt to collect:

- subscribers,
- email addresses,
- donations,
- payments,
- credentials,
- personal data,
- followers,
- community members,

by pretending to be the Official Project or by creating confusion about official ownership.

<br/>

### 4.3 Confusingly Similar Names

You may not register or use:

- domain names,
- GitHub organizations,
- GitHub repositories,
- GitBook spaces,
- social media accounts,
- newsletters,
- blogs,
- package names,
- community names,

that are deceptively similar to the protected identifiers.

Examples of prohibited confusing uses include, but are not limited to:

- “Maat-Cyber-World-Official”
- “Maat-Cyber-Official”
- “MaatCyberWorld”
- “Maat-Cyber-Tools”
- “Maat-Cyber-Training”
- “Maat-Cyber-Community”

where such use is not operated or authorized by the Author and is likely to confuse users.

<br/>

### 4.4 False Endorsement

You may not state or imply that Maat / Maat-Cyber-World endorses, sponsors, approves, or is affiliated with your project, product, course, service, platform, publication, or community unless explicitly authorized in writing.

<br/>

### 4.5 Phishing or Fraud

You may not use the project identity, content, or branding for phishing, fraud, scamming, credential harvesting, malware distribution, or any deceptive practice.

### Permitted

- Writing: “I learned from Maat-Cyber-World and used this methodology in my lab.”
- Linking to the official repository.
- Quoting a short portion with attribution where permitted by the content license.
